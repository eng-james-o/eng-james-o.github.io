Drone Conceptual Design Software

This project focuses on a simple software to assist in the conceptual design of drones, including performance analysis, propeller performance and design optimization.

Key Features:

  • Performance analysis of drone designs
  • Design optimization algorithms
  • Visualization of design parameters and performance metrics
