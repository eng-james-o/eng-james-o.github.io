UK Stock Market Forecasting

{% include image.html src="/assets/images/uk-stock-hero.png" alt="UK Stock Market Trends" caption="Forecasting the FTSE-100 index." %}

Problem

The stock market is notoriously volatile and difficult to predict due to the high noise and non-linearity of financial data. Traditional statistical models often fail to capture the complex temporal dependencies required for accurate forecasting.

Solution

I implemented a machine learning pipeline that utilizes hybrid deep learning architectures—specifically CNN-LSTM and GRU models. By incorporating 15 different financial indices as features, the model learns to identify patterns across multiple dimensions of market data.

Results

The hybrid CNN-LSTM model outperformed individual architectures by effectively extracting spatial features from input data before processing temporal sequences. The project achieved a significant improvement in directional prediction accuracy for the FTSE-100 index.

Tech Stack

  • Modeling: Keras, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn.
  • Data: Pandas, NumPy.
  • Visualization: Matplotlib.

Impact

This project demonstrates the power of hybrid deep learning in financial engineering, providing a framework for more robust predictive modeling in volatile markets.
