# AeroOps — AI-Enabled Aerial Operations Management

**Type:** Commercial software product · **Role:** Founder, product and engineering leadership · **Source:** Private

## Problem
Commercial UAV operations involve mission coordination, personnel and equipment records, safety workflows, maintenance, flight logs, and reporting. Fragmented tools make operational oversight and traceability difficult.

## Product direction
AeroOps is being developed as an integrated platform for planning and managing aerial operations across desktop, web, and mobile experiences. The mobile experience is intended to support field execution, data capture, equipment interaction, and synchronization—not merely read-only access.

## Architecture and engineering considerations
The platform spans user-facing applications, shared backend services, operational records, and AI/analytics initiatives. Core architectural questions include permissions, auditability, cross-device consistency, offline field work, and separation of operational decisions from AI-generated suggestions.

## My contribution
I lead product direction and coordinate architectural and engineering decisions across disciplines. This includes defining component boundaries, reviewing workflows, and aligning software development with actual drone-operational needs.

## Status and evidence
AeroOps is under active development. This overview is not a claim that every described module, AI capability, or external integration is production-ready. Capabilities should be verified individually before being represented as generally available.
