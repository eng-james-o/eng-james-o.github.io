Measuring forest loss & water-body change for carbon credits (MRV)

This project builds a reproducible pipeline in Google Earth Engine (JavaScript & Python/Colab) to detect and quantify deforestation and water surface dynamics across South Africa (with focus regions like KwaZulu‑Natal). Outputs feed ArcGIS Online maps & dashboards and support carbon credit monitoring, reporting, and verification (MRV).

Tech stack

  • Google Earth Python API
  • GeoTIFF/COG →ArcGIS Online

Why

Reliable measurement of forest cover change and water body extent is critical for nature‑based carbon projects and environmental stewardship. This work packages scalable EO methods so results are auditable and easy to operationalize.

Datasets

  • Sentinel‑1 (VV/VH) for cloud‑immune change cues & flood/water mapping.
  • Sentinel‑2 surface reflectance for NDVI/NDWI and land‑cover context.
  • Google Dynamic World yearly class probabilities (Trees class).
  • Hansen GFC tree cover baseline & annual loss reference.
  • Administrative boundaries (e.g., FAO GAUL) for province‑level stats.
  • Sentinel-5P OFFL CH4 for methane emission hotpots
  • JRC Monthly Water History for river network and dynamics
  • Landsat 8 TOA Reflectance

Methods

  • Cloud & shadow handling; SAR speckle‑aware compositing.
  • Annual composites (2015–2024) with Trees probability trends (Dynamic World).
  • Change logic: NDVI/NDMI deltas, DW class transitions, and S1 backscatter cues.
  • Vectorization of deforested patches with area thresholding (ha).
  • Random sampling points for QA/QC and ground‑truth planning.
  • Province‑level time‑series summaries for dashboards.

Deliverables & Exports

  • Raster: GeoTIFF / COG (deforestation, indices, water maps).
  • Vectors / Tables: GeoJSON / Shapefile / CSV (polygons, samples, stats).
  • Time‑series: CSV of yearly/ provincial metrics (2015–2024).
  • Dynamic tiles: Earth Engine map tiles for quick previews.
  • ArcGIS: Hosted imagery/feature layers → Dashboard cards & charts.

Pipeline at a glance

  1. Ingest & Preprocess — Filter by province boundary, dates; mask clouds; SAR prep.
  2. Composites — Annual mosaics; Trees probability means; NDVI/NDWI stacks.
  3. Change Detection — Difference maps + class transition rules.
  4. Post‑processing — Morphological cleaning; vectorization; min‑area filters.
  5. Validation — Random points, stratified by change/no‑change.
  6. Exports — COGs, GeoJSON/CSV, tile service, and dashboard‑ready summaries.

Tech notes

  • Earth Engine in JS (Code Editor UI) and Python (Colab/geemap).
  • Tile‑wise processing to reduce memory & improve responsiveness.
  • COG + (optional) titiler for fast web visualization; ArcGIS Pipeline for hosting.
  • Exports scripted for repeatable runs & audit trails.
