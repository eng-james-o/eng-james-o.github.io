SA Deforestation Monitoring

{% include image.html src="/assets/images/deforestation-map-sa.png" alt="Deforestation Map South Africa" caption="Deforestation hotspots in South Africa (2015-2025)." %}

Problem

Monitoring large-scale environmental changes like deforestation and water depletion is challenging due to the vast areas involved and the need for frequent, high-resolution data. Traditional ground surveys are insufficient for real-time policy and conservation decisions.

Solution

I developed an automated pipeline using Google Earth Engine and satellite data (Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, Landsat) to monitor deforestation, water body changes, and carbon emissions across South Africa. The system uses machine learning (Random Forest) to predict carbon density and track losses over time.

Impact

This project provides actionable intelligence for nature-based carbon projects and water resource management, helping South Africa meet its environmental stewardship goals and support carbon credit programs.
