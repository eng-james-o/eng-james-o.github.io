## The story

In 2021, I set out to build a fixed-wing surveillance UAV as proof of concept for an autonomous fixed-wing UAV with internet datalink, following Peter Burke's framework. One of the biggest challenges I faced was the lack of specialised equipment and knowledge gaps. During the project, my teammates, Babtunde Oladipupo and Oluwajuwon Folorunso and I, had to embark on multiple side projects in designing and building tools such as custom sized drill bits, hot wire styrofoam cutter, etc. While these afforded us the opportunity to broaden our knowledge, it speaks volumes about why it is quite uncommon to see other young individuals like us buuld advanced systems to tackle local problems snd compete globally. 

As I studied, took certification courses and widened my network, I reaslised that I had touched a fundamental problem in the African space - the problem of opportunity. I then set out to maximise what I had learned in the drone space to build solutions as well as to create a hub that enables researchers to build innovative solutions as well.

This is the vision and story behind Aerohub. 

## What do we do?

Currently, Aerohub offers consultancy to academic researchers in the drone space, in design, building, and deployment.
